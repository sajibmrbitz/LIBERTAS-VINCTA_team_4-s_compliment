SENSORY AI

The literal finite-state machine contains:
  WANDER_BLIND
  PATROL_AUDIO
  INVESTIGATE
  HUNT_AUDIO
  PATROL_SIGHT
  CHASE
  INVESTIGATE_LAST_SEEN
  PREDICT_HUNT
  AMBUSH

Stage 0 wanders for 8-15 seconds per target and cannot catch Els. Stage 1 escalates
two audible pings inside six seconds into HUNT_AUDIO. Stage 2 confirms vision for
0.6 seconds and breaks a lost chase after a random 5-8 seconds. Stage 3 retains five
locations, pre-seeds used hiding spots on Memory pickup, checks hiding priorities
with repeated use capped at High, and performs a 35 percent exit-ambush check every
three seconds.
