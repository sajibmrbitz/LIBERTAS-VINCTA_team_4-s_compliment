# LIBERTAS VINCTA - Narrative Canon

Team 4's Compliment. Development reference: contains spoilers; never render this document on player-facing pages.

The final title is **LIBERTAS VINCTA**. The older SENSES Narrative Bible is source material, not a replacement title. This document combines the supplied canon with current GameJam implementation. Narrative intentions below do not imply that final environment art or every breadcrumb is already installed.

## 1. Core Premise

Els Vantree wakes inside Hollowmere Estate after accepting a private appraisal job. Recovering her tools and opening locks restores her ability to act. Later, opening three seals restores capacities to something confined within the estate. Freedom changes hands.

## 2. Els Vantree

Els is a locksmith hired for an appraisal connected to Hollowmere. She is practical and skilled with locks. Do not invent childhood, spouse, family biography or unrelated history. Her deeper family connection to the estate is a mystery to unfold later.

## 3. What Els remembers

The Hollowmere job, an unusually generous fee, signing the contract, and failing to properly read the addendum.

## 4. What Els cannot remember

Walking through the front door, the events after signing/arriving, and approximately the last six hours. Do not resolve the missing hours in the intro.

## 5. Hollowmere Estate

The Cold Foyer, 2:47 AM: rain outside, dark/boarded windows, a dying wall sconce, cold floor and small house sounds breaking the silence. Els wakes collapsed, face toward the floor. Her flashlight lies several feet away, its beam pointed away from her. She cannot explain its position.

Current environment combines imported tiled floors, paneling, framed windows and furniture with the existing foyer geometry and dropped-beam placeholder. Final boarded-window and wall-sconce art remains presentation work. Audio hooks retain their current assignments.

## 6. The Ledger / Conservation of Freedom

Freedom is not destroyed; it is relocated. Hearing, Sight and Memory were taken from The Deprived One and bound into three seals/keys. Every restoration grants the entity a Degree of Freedom while removing a corresponding safe freedom from Els. Teach this mechanically, not through intro exposition.

## 7. The Deprived One

A contained entity whose Hearing, Sight and Memory were bound. Hearing enables response to noise. Sight enables recognition/exploitation of exposure. Memory enables learned patterns and prediction. Restoring Memory should make it feel more deliberate and less animal-like. Do not add an origin, identity or complete containment explanation beyond supplied canon.

## 8. Hearing chapter

Ground floor: Music Room, sealed piano, Vantree writings and Hearing Key. The piano interaction produces unavoidable noise. The Deprived One gains hearing; Els loses the freedom to make noise safely. Existing puzzle requirements, order, emitted noise and AI balance remain authoritative.

## 9. Sight chapter

Upper floor: Portrait Gallery, Master Bedroom and Nursery/passage. Portraits and writings suggest the Vantree connection. Terminology gradually shifts from Custodian toward Jailer without immediately explaining the truth. Restored Sight makes light and exposed space dangerous. Do not change current detection rules as part of narrative polish.

## 10. Memory chapter

Basement: Flooded Cellar, Wine Cellar and Ritual Chamber. Containment history becomes clearer. Memory restoration makes repeated hiding/routes less reliable; the entity's simple learning should carry narrative weight. Do not imply sophisticated intelligence beyond the implemented route/hiding memory.

## 11. Vantree / Custodian / Jailer mystery

Els's connection to Hollowmere runs deeper than she initially knows. Breadcrumb it through ancestral portraits, Vantree letters, Custodian/Jailer terminology and ritual architecture. Preserve the complete Jailer reveal for later discovery. The intended portrait/history breadcrumb is: "Forgive me, if you're reading this. It has to be someone." Recording it here does not claim it has been placed in a level.

## 12. Current GameJam endings

These are current code conditions, not newly rewritten outcomes:

- **Full Awakening:** exactly three keys; ground-floor front door. Els exits, and nothing remains deprived; the house listens.
- **Partial Mercy:** exactly two keys; basement maintenance exit. Els escapes through cold water, leaving the final seal holding.
- **Vantree:** at most one key and at least three letters; ground-floor custodian seal. Els leaves the Vantree seal intact and chooses to remain its custodian for now.

FreedomLedger.eligible and existing exit interactions own eligibility. No additional ending, post-ending scene or sequel explanation is introduced.

## 13. Spoilers forbidden on Story/Home pages

Do not explain which senses the keys contain, enemy mechanics, the Ledger's complete exchange law, the full Custodian/Jailer reveal, Els's full family connection, Memory-stage behavior, ending conditions/outcomes or sequel setup. The public Story page may establish the appraisal, contract/addendum, six-hour gap, three sealed keys and ambiguous ownership of freedom.

## 14. Intro dialogue canon

Opening, in this exact order, spoken by ELS:

1. "...Where am I?"
2. "Els Vantree... Hollowmere. The appraisal."
3. "They offered too much. I signed without reading the addendum."
4. "After that... nothing. Six hours just gone."

Then existing interaction dialogue:

- Flashlight: "Mine..." / "How did it get over there?"
- Tools: "At least I came prepared."
- First door attempt: "Locked." / "Of course."
- Missing tools: "I need my tools."
- Missing flashlight: "I should take my flashlight."
- Open doorway/corridor: "Hello?" No answer; enter Ground progression.

Do not add Ledger, senses, Custodian, Jailer, keys, monster or family-twist exposition to the opening memory sequence. Intro recovery uses presentation-only collapse frames, never death logic or death audio.

> Freedom is never destroyed.  
> It only changes hands.

> Forgive me, if you're reading this.  
> It has to be someone.

**Three keys. Three seals.** These are motifs, not instructions to display every phrase immediately.
